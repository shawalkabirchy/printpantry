Drafts for the owner. These describe a self-initiated studio project with synthetic records. No social posts have been published automatically. Verified public demo: [printpantry.pages.dev](https://printpantry.pages.dev/).

## 1. Problem post

A half-used filament spool leaves a small but useful question: is there enough for the next job?

For a workshop, the answer needs more than a rough percentage. It needs the job’s required grams, a reserve, and a current estimate of what is actually on the shelf.

That is the workflow behind PrintPantry, a self-initiated ConsoleLog Studio project: a local filament ledger connected to a simple job calculator.

The demo uses synthetic spools. I’m interested in the workflow itself: how do small print shops keep track of partial spools today?

## 2. Build-decision post

A new weighing should replace an estimate, not subtract the same print twice.

In PrintPantry, a 1,000 g opening balance minus 100 g consumption becomes 900 g. If the next measurement says 850 g remains, the new balance is 850 g.

Keeping those two actions distinct makes the inventory easier to trust and the history easier to explain.

The app also keeps mass and money in integer units, checks stale browser tabs before saving, and includes JSON backup recovery.

This is a self-initiated ConsoleLog Studio portfolio project, demonstrated with synthetic records. Small operational rules are often where an otherwise simple tool earns its usefulness.

## 3. Demo / case-study post

PrintPantry connects a workshop’s shelf to its next print.

In the demo, a 390 g job with a 10 g reserve initially has no suitable spool. A fresh weighing updates one spool to 400 g, and it becomes a match. The estimate charges the 390 g requirement; the reserve remains a safety buffer.

The working app includes inventory filters, a measurement ledger, consumption entries, phone layouts, and JSON backup/restore. Records stay in the browser and need backups; this release has no cloud sync or printer integration.

Built as a self-initiated ConsoleLog Studio project with synthetic data, not a commissioned client outcome.

If your team has a small workflow like this, I would be interested in how you handle it today: https://consolelog.studio/
